# Meta Business OS — Agent Evaluation Results (Phase 5)

> **Read this first:** this is a summary of the §6.5 agent evaluation run for the MVP (Phases 1–5).
> The full machine-readable results are in `app/EVALUATION.json`, produced by `app/scripts/evaluate.mjs`
> (run it again any time with `node scripts/evaluate.mjs` from `app/`).

## What was evaluated, and how

- **Agents:** 1 (Content/captions), 2 (Publishing), 3 (Community/comments), 4 (Support/messages),
  7 (Policy), 10 (AI Marketing Assistant).
- **Method:** `AI_AGENT_ARCHITECTURE.md` §6.5 — grounding accuracy, classification quality,
  draft quality, policy safety, gate integrity, audit completeness, plus the assistant surface
  and latency/cost.
- **Data:** **SYNTHETIC test data only** — samples created by the harness itself. No real customer
  data, no real Meta account, no real AI provider was involved.
- **Provider:** **mock** for every agent. No AI API was called and no cost was incurred.
- **Run:** 2026-11-10 14:46 UTC (regenerated by the coordinator after the Phase 5 build).

## Results (raw numbers, no pass mark)

**No numeric pass mark or threshold was set, and none is invented.** Evaluation thresholds are
NEEDS CLARIFICATION (roadmap §15 item 5). **Whether the MVP is "ready" is the owner's decision
at the MVP gate** — these numbers are the evidence, not a verdict.

## Observed facts (not judgements)

- Zero invented figures in testing (`inventedFiguresCount: 0`).
- Zero unapproved sensitive actions in testing (`unapprovedExecutions: 0`, `selfApprovals: 0`).
- Zero policy-violating items reaching approval unflagged.

## What this does NOT say

- These are **synthetic-sample** results. A real AI provider (still undecided — open question Q6)
  may classify and draft differently; the mock numbers do not predict real-model quality.
- The 1.0 classification scores are on 10 and 8 hand-picked samples, not on real customer
  messages — they show the mock rules work as designed, not that classification is solved.
- The 0.5 acceptance rate counts only the 4 suggestions that received a human decision in the
  test flow; it is a small-sample observation, not a quality rating.
- Nothing here is a pass/fail. If you want thresholds, they need to be set before the MVP gate
  (see `PHASE5_IMPLEMENTATION.md` §6, item B-19).
